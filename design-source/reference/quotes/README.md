## What they have in common
Trade-grouped works with area sub-headings; amounts as the one money column (unit rates optional); "FOC" / "Inclusive" / lump sums; per-section subtotals or page totals; GST 9 % and a grand total; terms and a payment schedule; a signature block — often on every page; a studio footer with registration numbers. Where they differ is everything visual: the letterhead, the labels, the bands, the borders, the faces, the ornaments. That is what the editor's canvases, styles and font list are for.
