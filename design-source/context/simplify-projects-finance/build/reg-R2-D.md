| REG-1545 (REG-R2-D.2) | Projects › Files & notes › Activity (and the lead's Activity) | **Each client ask gets its own Activity line.** The lines read "{who} made a task from {client}’s message — “{title}”", "{who} made a task from {client}’s ticket T-2026-004 — “{title}”" and "{who} raised VO-n from {client}’s message / ticket T-… — “{title}”". The client is whoever wrote it: the client, or someone from their household by name. For a ticket the studio raised, it is the lead's name. A line from a message sits under the **Messages** chip and a line from a ticket under the **Tickets** chip. The link opens the task (*See Task*) or the order in its editor (*Open VO-n*; on the project's Activity, Back returns there). If the task or order is gone, the link opens the message (*See Messages*) or the ticket (*See Ticket*) instead. A viewer who may not see the project's money doesn't see the order's line. | REG-1546 (REG-R2-D.3) | Settings › Notifications › Messages to clients · the client's email / WhatsApp | **A new client message: "A payment is due today".** It is a twelfth client event (`client_payment_due`, Money section). Email and WhatsApp are both **off by default**, and Owners and Admins can switch each on and edit the words like the other eleven. Once it is switched on, the client hears about each sent invoice **once, on its due day**, if some of it is still to pay. `{amount}` is what is left after payments that count, a client's "I have paid" still waiting to be confirmed, and issued credit notes. It goes through the delivery layer to the same people the invoice-sent email reaches: the client and their household who were invited and not withdrawn. Nothing goes while the portal is paused or Money is switched off, or to a client who was never invited. It never goes for a Draft or void invoice, a paid one, one on a deleted project or lead, or one sent that same day (the invoice-sent email already went that day). It is worked out when the studio's bell is read, like the bell's own reminders, and the outbox key `c:client_payment_due:<invoice>:<person>:<channel>` stops it repeating. It is listed under Sent messages as "To the client · A payment is due today". — her "fix all the pending things" 2026-10-09; P3 §6.8's option "a reminder on the due day" (the README's "Not built … a due-day reminder email"). *(invented copy: the row "A payment is due today" · "On a sent invoice’s due day, while some of it is still to pay — once for each invoice."; the email "Your {invoice} payment is due today" / "Hi {client},\n\nA reminder that your {invoice} invoice is due today — {amount} to pay.
