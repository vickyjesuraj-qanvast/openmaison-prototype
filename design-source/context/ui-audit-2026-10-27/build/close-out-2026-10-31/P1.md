## What was built

### C-04 — a restore moves the date inside the demo's signed contracts — BUILT
- **Why it drifted.** R50 kept each demo contract's copy as it read on its signing day. Every restore then moved `contracts.contract_date`
  with the demo day. The copy (`signed_body`) kept the day it was made. The base of 2026-10-26 printed "22 July 2026" on a contract dated
  3 August: 12 days apart, one for each restore-and-snapshot since R50. The lane DB was 13 days apart.
- **The restore** (`lib/db/demo-day.ts`): a new `EMBEDDED` kind, `fields`, on `contracts.signed_body`. It moves only the days a kept copy's
  fields print from the records (`DATE_FIELDS`: `date.contract` · `date.today` · `quote.validUntil` · `project.completedOn` ·
  `project.defectsUntil`). Each moves by the same whole days as its record and stays in its own words ("22 July 2026"). Every other word
  stays as signed (`shiftDateFields`, plus `fieldDay` to read one). The file head's "what was signed keeps its words" now names this exception.
- **The fingerprint** (`lib/db/demo-fingerprints.ts`, new, pure, server/scripts only): `refingerprint(before, after)` builds `document_hash`
  again over the copy each contract now holds. It uses the one way signing builds it (`documentHashOf`). A signed set's `signing_hash` follows
  its documents (`setHashOf`, in the set's reading order, as `signBundleOnline` builds it). It changes only a fingerprint that was built that
  way and leaves any other one alone. `lib/db/baseline.ts` `restoreBaseline` calls it after `shiftDemoRows`.
- **Repair R180** brings back the copies that had already drifted. If a kept copy's Contract date field is not its contract's date (else its
  Today's date is not its signing day), R180 moves every printed day by that gap. The gap comes from the restores, so it is the same for every
  field. R180 then builds the fingerprint again and changes nothing else. It is idempotent and has two checks: "signed contract whose kept copy
  prints another date than its own" (the app's `longDate` spelled in SQL, local time) and "signed contract whose fingerprint is not its kept
  copy's" (worked out in JS).
- `lib/db/time-columns.ts` and `scripts/restore-baseline.ts` needed no change. `signed_body` is text, not a timestamp column, and the
  script calls `restoreBaseline`.
- Verified: a repaired base was snapshotted and restored 17 days later. All three copies are on their own date and the fingerprints are ok
  (`scratch/t/restore-test.mts`). R180 on the lane said "+13 days" ×3, then "every kept copy prints its own date". Shot
  `shots/signed-contract-date-1440.png` ("made on 4 August 2026", signed 8 Aug '26).
