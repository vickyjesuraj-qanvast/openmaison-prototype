## What was built

- **Data.** `quote_comparisons.deleted_at` (that one column, `lib/db/schema.ts`). The migration is **`drizzle/0116_xray_soft_delete.sql`**
  (`ALTER TABLE quote_comparisons ADD deleted_at integer`), generated with `drizzle-kit generate --name xray_soft_delete`, plus
  `drizzle/meta/0116_snapshot.json` and the journal line. It is additive. Restore and repair shift the column with the demo day by
  themselves (`lib/db/time-columns.ts` reads the schema).
- **Delete with Undo.** `deleteComparison(id)` keeps its name and answers, and it now stamps `deleted_at`. It logs `comparison_deleted`
  on the lead, now with the reading's `id`. Both confirms say where it goes: "{name} moves to Deleted comparisons, where you can restore
  it." The snackbar "{name} deleted" carries **Undo** (⌘Z presses it too), from Recent's row ⋮ and from the record's ⋮. The record lands
  on its X-Ray page as before.
- **Restore / Delete forever / Delete all forever** — `restoreComparisons(ids)`, `deleteComparisonsForever(ids)`,
  `emptyComparisonTrash(leadId)` and `listDeletedComparisons(leadId)` in `app/quote-x-ray/actions.ts`. Each one starts with the
  workspace refusal, then the Pro lock, then the delete's rule. A refusal of every row answers the rule (`{ error }`).
  - A restore right after its delete (within 2 min, the AI Visualizer's `UNDO_MS`) takes the delete's line away. A later restore logs
    `comparison_restored`.
  - Delete forever logs `comparison_purged`, then removes the rows.
- **Deleted comparisons sheet** — `components/xray/deleted-comparisons-sheet.tsx`, Deleted contracts' twin on its stylesheet. That is the
  house Deleted sheet:
  - the crumb is the head's eyebrow, Delete all forever is its one action, there is no foot;
  - the columns are Comparison · Your quote · Deleted · Made by · Actions;
  - each row has Restore / Delete forever, and the selection column comes with the bulk bar;
  - a failed load says so with a retry;
  - a refused row is disabled, with the rule in its tooltip;
  - on a phone it is `phoneFull` with the contract sheets' card rows (`data-card`).

  It opens from a new **⋮ on Recent comparisons' head** (row "Deleted comparisons", as a list's ⋮ holds its Deleted sheet). On Free the
  row opens the Pro lock, as Delete does. `?deleted=1` opens the sheet on arrival (from Activity), and closing it drops the mark. The
  head keeps its 53px height (8 + 36 + 8), and the setup ghost draws the ⋮ slot. Measured: page 1049,161 358×53 = ghost 1049,161 358×53
  at 1440.
- **Every read skips deleted rows:**
  - Recent (`fetchRecents`);
  - the record (`fetchComparison` → not found);
  - every other X-Ray action (`openComparison` answers a deleted one as gone);
  - ⌘K (`lib/global-search/queries.ts`);
  - the Lost dialog's competitor (`lib/leads/queries.ts` `lastComparison`, both reads);
  - a meeting's readings (`lib/meetings/queries.ts`).

  The deleted sheet honours Who sees leads, and a deleted lead's readings wait with the lead.

  **Kept on purpose:** Studio health (`lib/studio-health/sources.ts`) still counts the act of running X-Ray, as a deleted contract
  still counts "made a contract". The repair checks stay as they are.
- **Activity rule** (`lib/leads/activity.ts`, the comparison branch only, plus `lib/leads/events.ts`'s two kinds). It follows the house
  rule for deleted documents (a deleted quote's and contract's lines stay):
  - A deleted reading keeps its run and send lines. While it waits in Deleted they open **"See deleted comparisons"**
    (`/quote-x-ray?lead=…&deleted=1&from=…`), because its record answers not found.
  - `comparison_deleted` links there while the reading is still deleted.
  - New: `comparison_restored` — "{who} brought back the Quote X-Ray comparison “…”." It links to the reading while it is live.
  - New: `comparison_purged` — "{who} deleted the Quote X-Ray comparison “…” for good."
  - Glyph `scan` for all three.
  - On a project's Activity the two new kinds read as lead history, and the X-Ray page link gets the project's way back.
