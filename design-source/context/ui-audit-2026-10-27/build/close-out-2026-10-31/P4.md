### dash-d1 — the Dashboard's Your day ghost after a meeting's time — BUILT
- **Cause.** The ghost drew the demo morning's actions on fixed sample rows: Directions on the site visit and the Hangout, a ⋮ column
  always.
  - Once a meeting's time has passed, its row has no Directions and no Prepare me (`ended`, cards-queries.ts). So the words take the room,
    and on a phone the row is a line shorter: the 24px.
  - After 3 PM no shown row has a ⋮, so the page drops the ⋮ column.
  - On a fresh base restored today, 390t was day 544 vs 520.
- **Seed or memory?** I took the page's memory over the seed:
  - The ghost already draws Your day's rows from what the page drew last (`CARD_COUNT_KEYS.day`). Computing the day in the shell's seed
    (`lib/ghost-shapes.server.ts`) would mean every page load reads the member's meetings, Hangouts and calls before the first byte (P-14).
  - So the page now also tells its next loader **each row's action** and **whether the rows kept a ⋮ column**:
    - one rule, `dayActOf` / `dayExtrasOf` in `lib/dashboard/cards.ts`, used by the card itself and by the memory;
    - remembered as `om.count.dashboard.day.acts` (base-3 per row: none · Directions · Call) and `om.count.dashboard.day.menus`.
  - The ghost's rows draw those actions: Directions 70; Call as the 62 text button at a desk, the 36 glyph on a phone (it was an 84 outlined
    box at every width).
  - A browser that hasn't seen the page draws the demo morning's, as before.
- **Found and fixed with it — the greeting.** On a Saturday at 390 the page's line "Saturday 31 October · 4 new since Wednesday" wraps to two
  lines. The ghost's "Monday 26 October · 4 new since Friday" doesn't, so every region below stood 20 lower.
  - The page now remembers its line count on a phone (`om.count.dashboard.hello.lines.phone`).
  - The ghost puts the news on the next line when the page's did (`.helloSinceWrap`, phone only).
- **Result:** dash-d1 § 8 all green at 1440 and 390t on a fresh base (every region +0/+0), plus a new check that the ghost's rows carry the
  page's actions.
  - § 9 (P-14 speed) fails only under `next dev` (1.2 s first byte). It needs the production build.
  - Reset prototype to base clears these `om.*` keys like the others.
