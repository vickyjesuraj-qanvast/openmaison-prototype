# C — Insights and the numbers: Lead ↔ Project parity (2026-10-08)

**What was compared.** Company › Reports › **Leads** (`/company/reports/leads`, `components/leads/insights/insights-view.tsx`, REG-611 → REG-1361)
against Company › Reports › **Projects** (`/company/reports/projects`, `components/projects/insights/project-insights-view.tsx`, gaps build I,
REG-1332), with Reports › **Finance** (`/company/reports/finance`, `components/finance/books/reports-view.tsx`) where it shows the same kind of
thing. Also the numbers on each record: the lead's LEAD STAGES card (`components/leads/detail/stages-card.tsx`) against the project's Status,
Money and Closing summary cards (`components/projects/project-overview.tsx`). The formatters checked are `formatTat`, `daysWords`, `daysLabel` / `days`,
`stageAge`, `money2`, `moneySGD`, `moneyCompact` and `money`.

**How.** A production build on :3011 against a scratch copy of the data, signed in as Dane (Owner). Playwright at 1440×1024, at 768×1024, and at
390×844 with touch. Every range chip was pressed. The Maison AI sheet was opened and its suggestions pressed. The loaders were captured with
JavaScript off. Each record named in the brief was opened. Nothing was saved.
Screenshots are in `scratchpad/lpaudit/shots/C/`, named by finding (`C-nn-lead.png` / `C-nn-project.png`, and `-finance` where Finance is the third side).
The full pages are `reports-{leads,projects,finance}-{1440,768,390}.png`. Measurements are in `scratchpad/lpaudit/c1-reports.json` and `c2-records.json`.

**Short version.** The project page is built on the lead page: it composes the lead's stylesheet, `StatTile`, `Chips`, the table core and Column
settings, so the frame matches. The differences are in **the numbers**:
- The same quantity is written in three duration formats: "12d", "12 days" and "5 weeks".
- Days are counted three ways. One project reads **50 days** on its record and **51 days** in the report.
- A tile labelled "Average duration" shows the median.
- Each page lays out its tile row differently at 768 and 390.
- The lead page shows a row of chips for work that is waiting. The project page has no such row and folds "late now" into a tile's help line.
- Maison AI answers on Projects and says *Coming soon* on Leads.
- The words for lateness change from one place to the next: "overdue", "late", "running late" and "done late".

## 3. Questions for Vicky

These are only the cases where the right answer is not simply "the lead's pattern" and no decision is on record.

1. **Q-1 · One duration format (C-01).** Leads uses 1.0's compact "30m · 26.3h · 12d" (REG-611 kept it). The mock and both record cards use words
   ("2 days"), and Projects uses words with weeks past 8 weeks. *Recommendation:* "n days" in words everywhere, never weeks. Under two days keep
   "30m" and "26.3h", since turnaround needs them. The listing's "5d in Met" becomes "5 days in Met".
2. **Q-2 · One day count (C-02).** Lead records count whole 24-hour spans; project records count calendar days (REG-1131). *Recommendation:* calendar
   days for every "n days", because studios think in days on the calendar and the Schedule needs it. The report then counts the same way.
3. **Q-3 · The tile row on a tablet and a phone (C-04).** *Recommendation:* one `StatTiles` rule. Up to 5 in one row ≥1025. At 768–1024 never
   below about 176 px (3 + 2). On a phone, two per row as the Money tab and the projects listing do, with labels wrapping. Leads' three would then read 2 + 1 on a phone,
   or keep one column if Vicky prefers that for three.
4. **Q-4 · Ranges (C-05).** Should both turnaround reports open on the same default? Projects opens on 90 days, perhaps because 30 days rarely contains a
   finished project. Should Leads gain *Last 12 months*? And should Finance keep calendar periods (This month · This quarter · This year…) beside the
   rolling windows? *Recommendation:* the same set and default for Leads and Projects (90 days; *Last 12 months* on both). Finance keeps calendar periods,
   because money is reported by month, quarter and year.
5. **Q-5 · A project's head figure (C-17).** REG-1163 settled the completed head. For a running project, keep "Started n days ago", which repeats the
   *In progress* row, or take the lead's grammar, the record's age ("n days since Won", "n days since added" for a direct project)?
6. **Q-6 · Grading.** The lead grades its outreach leg (good ≤ 8 h · warn ≤ 24 h · bad, from 1.0). Projects grades only *Late milestones* > 0. Should
   *Average duration* be graded against the plan, as the Closing summary compares actual and planned days, and *Collection days* against the
