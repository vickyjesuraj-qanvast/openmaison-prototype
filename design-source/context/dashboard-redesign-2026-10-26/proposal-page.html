OpenMaison 2.0 · the Dashboard, redesigned · for Vicky

The front door, made to be used every morning

The studio opens this page first, every day. Today it reads like a report. This proposal turns it into the place the day is run from: what needs you, how the studio is doing, what's coming, with Maison AI docked under it like a chat input. Mark each proposal and question; nothing is built until you do.

Drawn with the demo studio's records, Mon 26 Oct; figures as the demo shows themStudies: the page today · every module's signals · 17 home screens and 9 assistant inputs · 1.0's chat and its components
What's wrong today, measured

A page of sentences, and almost nothing happens on it

410 : 87words to figures on the Owner's page. 92% of the first screen's text is 12–14px, 59% of it grey.
37 of 45presses leave the page. Only ticking a task acts where you are.
850pxfrom the Ask box to where its answer opens, in a 320px corner popover. On a touch tablet, mid-page, there's no way to ask.
3 vs 2overdue follow-ups, in two cards on one page. New leads read 4 and 5; lead totals 31, 30 and 21.
8 timesthe register logs the floating Maison AI button covering a control.
1 of 6signals from Quotes, Contracts and the portal reach the page: quote approvals. A client opening a quote three times never does.

What works and stays: the ranked list of what needs you, one home per thing with ?from= and Back, every figure taken from its module's own count, role scoping, links that open the step itself, tick with Undo, and Maison AI's answers from the records.

The idea

Three questions, in the order an owner asks them

first

What needs me now?

One ranked list across every module, each row with its verb on it. Act in place, see what it did, Undo. The day can be finished.

at a glance

How is the studio doing?

Six figures in one strip, each with its comparison and a small trend. Graded where a grade exists. A press opens its report.

around it

What changed, what's coming?

A "since you were last here" line, your day and the week, the pipeline, the projects, the team, the site. Each card answers one question.

Maison AI stops being a card you scroll past. It's a field docked at the foot of the page, the way a chat input sits under a conversation, with three suggestions drawn from today's records. Its answers come back as small interactive cards, the best of what 1.0's chat returned, each ending in a door.

What answers are made of

The best of 1.0's chat answers, rebuilt on the house pieces, each ending in a door to the record.

  • Figures: two to four tiles with their comparison
  • A record: a lead or project card with its facts and Open
  • A list you can act on: rows with their verb, the same as Needs you
  • A small chart: bars or a line, with values on hover or tap
  • A timeline: what happened on a record, by day, with type icons
  • A comparison: this vs that, side by side
  • A receipt: what Maison AI did, with Undo
  • Doors: one press to the place; repeats fold into "Open … · 4"

Not brought from 1.0: the endless chip marquee, the page swapping into a chat, three chat surfaces that disagreed, writes without confirmation, invented market figures.

Interactions

What a press, a hover and a key do

Acting in place

  • The verb on a row acts at once and shows a receipt with Undo (5 s), then the row leaves and the ring fills.
  • Review opens a pane (desk) or a sheet (phone) with the record and its approve / send back.
  • ⋯ on a row: Open, Not today (back tomorrow), Ask about this.
  • Call and WhatsApp open straight from the row; the call is remembered so "Log the call" is offered on return.

Glancing

  • Hover on a Pulse figure shows its breakdown (by source, by age, by project) and Ask.
  • A day in the week strip shows that day's agenda in place.
  • Chips in "Since you were last here" open the thing and tick off.
  • Red only for what's ours and late; a client's lateness stays neutral.

Keys and motion

  • J / K move through Needs you, Enter acts, R reviews, ⌘J focuses Maison AI.
  • Rows ease out when cleared; the ring fills; nothing else moves on its own.
  • When all is clear: "You're all caught up", the ring full, and what's coming next.
  • The page refreshes when you come back to it, never under your cursor.

Stitched together

One fact, one number, everywhere

What the page needs fixed underneath

  • One clock for "overdue": the bell counts from midnight, My tasks from the hour. One rule, used by both.
  • One "Owed to you": today it means three different amounts on three pages.
  • One tickets count: counted five ways and named three.
  • One "not contacted": four rules today. And "Needs attention" means four things.
  • Two profits named apart: the studio's month and a project's margin.
  • Money on cards as "S$ 77,900"; dates one way; "5 days overdue", never "(5d)".
  • Every door carries ?from=/dashboard; five don't today.
  • The page shows a module's own figure, never its own count of it.

Found on the way, to fix

  • A quote's follow-up task never closes when the quote is confirmed or signed.
  • Coming up cuts each project to six rows before merging, so a busy project hides the rest.
  • The bell rings for supplier bills still waiting for approval.
  • The greeting uses the server's clock; on a UTC server it says "Good morning" at 3 PM. Pin it to the studio's time.
  • The page reads every project in full and starts answering in 0.29 s (Leads: 0.08 s). Read only what it shows.
  • At 1024 with a mouse, the floating button covers the Finance row's Open.
