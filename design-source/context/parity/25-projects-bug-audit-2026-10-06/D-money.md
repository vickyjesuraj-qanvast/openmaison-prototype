**Covered:** the project's **Money** tab end to end against the Finance calls F-01…F-20 (build record §1): the story sentence, the four tiles
and whether they agree with the Overview's Money card, the listing's Contract value / Collected and the invoice / receipt pages; the Payment
schedule, Variation orders, Invoices sent and Payments tables (cells, row menus, next steps, Column settings); every banner (approval, sent
