# B · The Schedule — bug audit (2026-10-06)

**Covered:** the Schedule tab end to end against the Project Schedule Proposal canvas (all seven boards: Main · Moved · Share · Phone ·
Start · Milestone · Depth). That means the list that is also the timeline (Weeks / Months, Today, weekends, bars, legend, hover hints),
dragging a bar and its ends (Escape, snapping, followers, the sentence + Undo, the spring), the keyboard (bar keys, ⌘Z, J/K, the ? panel),
add, rename, move up/down and delete + Undo, the four statuses and Mark done, and the milestone panel with all eight layers. It also covers
Share changes (first share and later shares, the snapshot, What {client} sees, Something is not right → Ask again, the portal's copy), the
three ways to start (quote · template · blank) and Settings › Projects › Schedule defaults. Outside the tab: Company › Templates ›
Schedules (gallery, list, New ▾, editor, Used by), the bell's milestone rows, attention reasons on the listing and Overview, the
Overview's Schedule card, the Tasks tab's Milestone column and filter, the listing's Next milestone / Progress, a Completed project and
the Activity lines.

**As whom:** Dane (Owner), Michelle (Designer on Ryan's and Aisha's team), Sarah (Designer on no team, no money access) and Benjamin Han
in the portal (fresh context, 97731968). Managers were read from the code (`lib/projects/policy.ts`).

**Widths:** 1440×1024, 1025×800, 768×1024 touch, 390×844 touch and 320 (overflow). Data came from a copy of `local.db` on :3102,
re-copied between runs. Projects: Benjamin Han P-2026-002, Ryan D'Souza P-2026-001, Aisha Rahman P-2026-003 (never shared; also with
her milestones soft-deleted on the copy to test Start), Marcus Teo P-2026-004 (no schedule) and Shalini Menon P-2025-001 (completed).
`local.db` was never written. The server log stayed clean (no errors).

Shots: `shots/B/` (prefix `d-` 1440, `m-` 1025, `t-` 768 touch, `p-` 390, `x-` 320).
