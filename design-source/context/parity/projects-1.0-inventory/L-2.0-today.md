## Bottom line

- **Projects in 2.0 today is a name with nothing behind it:** an inert rail item, an inert phone-dock item, an inert Settings tab, and a Won step whose button reads *Save & Convert to Project* and whose snackbar reads "Project created for {lead} · OM-n" — while no record is created. No route, no table, no loader, no ghost, no mock (L-01…L-13, L-22…L-24, L-32).
- **The Won step already collects most of what a project starts from** — the signed quote, the signed contract, a four-milestone payment split that must total 100 %, a project type (three values) — but it keeps them as one JSON payload on a `stage_logs` row, by the documents' *names*, not their ids (L-14…L-21).
- **One press is the hand-off point:** a Won lead's *Convert to Project* and the snackbar's *View* both answer "Coming soon in OpenMaison 2.0" (`app/leads/[id]/overview.tsx:247`, `:644`; `app/leads/workspace.tsx:610`).
- **The schema has hooks waiting:** `project_rooms.project_id` and `render_jobs.project_id` (null "until Projects"), `homeowner_lead_links` ("a link is to a lead until Projects exist"), `quotes.project_ref`, `lead_tasks.lead_id` nullable. Everything else a project would show — quotes, contracts, tasks, files, notes, questions, designs — hangs off `leads.id`, mostly with cascade delete (L-32…L-37, section 4).
- **Earlier audits pushed some fifty items to Projects, Finance, the portal, the Dashboard and Meetings** (48 rows below) — the project record itself, commission, the project team, project types, the Gantt, timeline / milestone templates, variation orders, contract value and freeze, invoices / POs, portal signing and sharing, Photo Renders on a project, metered project reads (section 2).
- **The lead detail page is the record-page pattern** to build on: `PageHeaderL2` + `Tabs` + a collapsible info column + two card stacks + the Create strip, one route serving every tab, one ghost per tab, `?from=` kept across tabs (section 3).
- **House pieces cover a listing and a record page** (table model, phone cards, kanban, `StatTile`, `Chips`, `ScopePill`, bulk bar, export, column settings, drawer, dialogs, folders, empty states, ghosts). **Nothing exists for** a Gantt / timeline, a calendar, a chart, a progress ring or a workload view, and `package.json` carries no chart or date-grid library (section 5; L-285…L-294).
- **No Projects, Dashboard, Finance, Meetings or portal mock is named anywhere** in `DESIGN.md` or the register; the only Figma evidence is the nav items themselves and the *Convert to Project* button on the lead header. Under the house rules every Projects surface is therefore "no mock": proposed first, built on house pieces, logged in the register (L-197…L-200, L-300…L-304).
- **Standing decisions a Projects build would contradict unless the owner rules:** "no Archive" for leads and templates (REG-502, REG-605) against 1.0's Archived projects; `G P` already goes to the Price book; task-due notices stop once a lead is Won; the Usage page says "projects never use AI credits"; Plans already promises "Gantt tracking & homeowner portal" and "Floor plan coordination"; deleting a lead has no Won / project guard; *Who sees leads* is decided by a lead's assignees (L-10, L-31, L-149, L-152, L-201, L-256, L-259).
- **Motion is a closed token set** (`lib/motion.ts`): 50 / 150 / 150-100 / 200-140 / 280-180 ms, three easings, two springs, six variants; reduced motion is handled at the root. A Gantt, a ring or a count-up would be new motion and needs the owner (section 7).
- **Demo seeds exist:** three Won leads — Benjamin Han (OM-10005, S$69,283.13), Ryan D’Souza (OM-10004, S$77,900.12), Aisha Rahman (OM-10009, S$62,702.25) — each with a Signed quote, a Signed contract and a 10/30/30/30 schedule, but with no tasks, files, notes, questions or designs; Aisha's Won log is dated five days before her signature (section 9).
- **The register is at REG-906, the repair rules at R67, the migrations at 0057** — the next free numbers a Projects build would take.

## 4 · Existing records a project would inherit or link

**Quotes and payments**

- **L-115** · `quotes` belong to a lead (`lead_id`, cascade) and carry number, title, `amount_minor`, visibility, status, due / sent / approved / signed stamps, version, template pin, General Info (prepared by, valid until, project ref, NRIC, customer id, property type, site address), pricing controls, the issuing entity, a second client, the approval record (`lib/db/schema.ts:248-324`).
- **L-116** · Quote statuses: Draft · Sent for Approval · Approved · Sent to Client · Confirmed · Signed (`lib/leads/quote-filters.ts:10`; *Confirmed* joined with REG-587). `signed_at` is set when the contract raised from it is signed, or Signed is picked by hand (`schema.ts:320-321`; REG-626).
- **L-117** · **There is no "contract value" record.** A quote's amount is always derived from its lines (`DESIGN.md:109`); the Won payload holds a typed amount only for an uploaded quote (`stage-data.ts:42`).
- **L-118** · `quote_payments` is the payment schedule: name, description, `pct`, `due_kind` (approval · estimate · fixed), `due_at`. **Amounts are derived, never stored** — total × share (`schema.ts:422-444`; `page-checklist.md:203`; REG-418). There is no paid / invoiced state on a row.
- **L-119** · Estimated due dates hang from the lead's estimated start date (else three weeks after the quote was written) and a **renovation length by property type** — HDB 3-Room 8 weeks … Landed 20, default 12 (`lib/quotes/payments.ts:78-87`). This is the only duration model in 2.0.
- **L-120** · `payment_templates` are the studio's splits — the four presets `default` (10/30/30/30) · `20-40-40` · `30-70` · `50-50` plus the studio's own, in folders (`schema.ts:403-420`; REG-538).
- **L-121** · `quote_versions` keep each sent version whole; `quote_items` hold lines and sections (with `room`, `category`, `section`, vendor, rebate, optional, client choice); `quote_files` link the lead's files; `quote_item_comments` are the team's line threads (`schema.ts:331-343`, `:504-553`, `:374-382`, `:977-990`).
- **L-122** · A quote's due date raises a *Follow up on quote OM-n* task kept in step with the date (REG-470).
- **L-123** · Quote X-Ray readings (`quote_comparisons`) hang off a lead and a quote with `set null` on delete (`schema.ts:594-620`).

**Contracts and signing**

- **L-124** · `contracts` belong to a lead (cascade): title, status Draft · Sent to Client · Signed, the composed body or an uploaded file's name, recipient, `from_quote_id`, issuing entity, automation switches, the signed copy and its SHA-256 fingerprint, the signing record, actor stamps (`schema.ts:636-694`; REG-534, REG-631…634).
- **L-125** · `contract_bundles` send several documents under one link and one signature (`schema.ts:718-738`; REG-591).
- **L-126** · Contract Automation raises **tasks** — `lead_tasks.contract_id` — after sending and after signing (`lib/contracts/automation.ts`; REG-634).
- **L-127** · A **Variation Order** exists only as a contract *template* in the demo — "Variation Order" in Templates › Contracts › *Variations* (`fixtures/baseline.json` `contract_templates`, `library_folders`; REG-665, repair R65). It is a document to sign, with no omission or credit arithmetic.
- **L-128** · Client-facing pages share one shell and no session: `/sign/[id]` and `/sign/bundle/[id]` (64 bar, paper + 360 card, code → name → sign), `/sign/[id]/quote`, `/confirm/[quoteId]`, `/renders/[token]`, and `/f/[token]` for a shared file (`app/sign/layout.tsx`, `app/confirm/layout.tsx`, `app/renders/layout.tsx`, `app/f/[token]/route.ts:13-22`; REG-534, REG-587, REG-676). Each has its own snackbar host and ghost.

**Tasks**

- **L-129** · `lead_tasks`: title, kind (General · Call · Follow-up), `due_at`, `done`, creator, description, seen stamp, template and contract links (`schema.ts:164-186`; `lib/leads/enums.ts:46`). A task has **no start date, duration, dependency, status beyond done, or milestone flag**.
- **L-130** · Around it: `tags` / `task_tags`, `task_assignees`, `task_comments` ("Clients can’t see these comments"), `task_attachments` (rows of the lead's files) (`schema.ts:934-1001`).
- **L-131** · `task_templates` / `template_tasks` are checklists a studio adds to a lead in one go; a step carries members, tags and `due_days` after it lands (`schema.ts:1004-1032`; REG-538). The demo has two templates, *Leads Template* and *Template 1*, in the folders *Lead follow-up* and *Meetings* (`fixtures/baseline.json`).
- **L-132** · The rail's Tasks badge is the viewer's own overdue tasks (`lib/leads/tasks.ts:104-113`; UX audit T5).

**Files, notes, questionnaire**

- **L-133** · `lead_files`: folder · image · pdf, nested by `parent_id`, owner, size, mime, soft delete; pictures live beside the row in `lead_file_images` and are served at `/files/img/<id>` (`schema.ts:763-788`; REG-684). ## 6 · Design rules that constrain a Projects build

**Who decides, and where it is written**

- **L-197** · **Nothing a user can see changes without Vicky** — layout, copy, icons, motion, flows, empty / loading / error states, who may do what, any new screen (`DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md:7-24`; `AGENTS.md`). With a developer, an assistant stops and offers a proposal or keeps the work out of the visible layer.
- **L-198** · **The mock wins; never invent UI, copy or data.** Where no mock exists: propose first; when told to build, log it in `design-source/REGISTER.md` with its source — mock · user-directed · invented copy · bug fix (`CLAUDE.md` › How we work 1; `DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md:39-46`).
- **L-199** · **The parity programme's ground rules** (owner, 2026-09-24): features, functions, logic and data only — **no 1.0 UI**; mismatches listed apart; numbered questions; **nothing from an audit is built until the owner marks it** (REG-605; `CLAUDE.md` › Map, the `design-source/` row).
- **L-200** · **An unmocked surface is built on house pieces**: "Before building an unmocked surface, find the closest built pattern and reuse its piece" (`page-checklist.md:95`; REG-614; owner: "align to 2.0 with our DS and UI components", `DESIGN.md:122`).
- **L-201** · **Archive is a standing question.** Leads keep "Linear + Delete" with Journey mode and Archive excluded (REG-605); templates "delete softly… No Archive" (REG-502; `page-checklist.md:217` "Never an Archive"); yet quotes and contracts do have Archive and *Archived Quotes / Archived Contracts* sheets (REG-175, REG-656…660). 1.0's Draft · Active · Closed · Archived project statuses need the owner's call against this.
- **L-202** · **No mock data.** Every value on a screen is produced by a record; Figma strings and figures are layout direction only (REG-403; `page-checklist.md:195`).
- **L-203** · **Demo data must be internally consistent** — every state shown is backed by the records that produce it; run `npm run db:repair` after any seeding, then `npm run db:baseline`; never edit the JSON by hand (REG-343; `CLAUDE.md`). A new table must join both baseline lists (`lib/db/baseline.ts:17`, `scripts/snapshot-baseline.ts:6`), child after parent.
- **L-204** · **The next free numbers**: the register ends at REG-906, repair rules at R67 (`scripts/repair-demo-data.ts:1749`), migrations at `0057_ai_credits.sql`. Migrations are additive only.

**System**

- **L-205** · Tokens only (`--om-*`); radii 4 fields and buttons · 8 cards · 12 modals; elevation levels 1…5; type through `--om-type-*`; DS components only through `@/components/ds` (`DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md:48-58`; `DESIGN.md:61-63`).
- **L-206** · Modal scale s 400 · m 600 · l 800 · xl 1200, xxl fluid with a 48 margin; height to content; **no full-page overlays** (`page-checklist.md:65`; `DESIGN.md:64`).
- **L-207** · Every overlay wears the one head and foot; never a head, close or foot of your own (`page-checklist.md:66`; REG-725).
- **L-208** · Snackbar: 344×48, radius 4, one action in `#CCB2FF`; 4 s plain, 8 s with an action; never the only place a must-read lives; every inert affordance answers "Coming soon in OpenMaison 2.0" (`DESIGN.md:74-75`; REG-898).
- **L-209** · **One ghost per page**, drawn by both `loading.tsx` and `PageReveal`, on the page's own layout; a loader wraps its own page only (route groups for parents); a first-run page uses `GhostShape` (`page-checklist.md:6-26`, `:84`; REG-708, REG-817).
- **L-210** · **Empty states — six rules**: a first run carries its action; one filled button on the screen; nothing that can't act (search, Filters, pills, views hide); say it once; a sample is a door; "No results found" only after a search or a filter (`page-checklist.md:76-84`; REG-735…737).
- **L-211** · **Tables follow the leads listing**; two table types only (the listing's model, the card table); a table of records is cards at ≤767; a selection column is always visible; sort glyphs only on columns that order a list; a new table gets Column settings in the same change (`DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md:110-116`; `page-checklist.md:159`, `:198`; REG-430, REG-454, REG-468, REG-727).

- **L-213** · **Pill or Filters**: one dominant single-choice dimension → a scope pill; several or multi-select → the Filters modal; never both for one dimension; a narrowed view wears the dot or the count (`DESIGN.md:91-92`; REG-295, REG-297). Work waiting on a person is a to-do chip whose count is the viewer's own (`page-checklist.md:88`).

- **L-215** · **Icons** are a self-hosted subset; a name outside `public/fonts/ICONS.txt` renders as the literal word and needs a re-subset (`CLAUDE.md` › Icons; REG-207).

**Behaviour**

- **L-216** · **Fields**: every input is `TextField`; numbers filtered as typed; dates never typed; validation shown after the first submit, clearing live, messages from `MSG`; required fields starred through `required`; anything saved opens read-only with an explicit Edit (`page-checklist.md:106-122`; REG-150, REG-451).

- **L-218** · **Say why**: a refused action answers `{ error: <the rule> }` and the screen shows that rule; an optimistic write is put back when the server refuses; a dialog closes only once its save lands (`CONTRIBUTING.md:89-91`; REG-656…660).
- **L-219** · **Hand-offs**: a document that changes state offers the next step right there — snackbar, banner and bell line — and never moves anything silently (`page-checklist.md:87`; REG-738…818).
- **L-220** · **Back returns where you came from** — every list-to-record link carries `?from=`; a crumb never repeats itself (`DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md:85-90`; REG-465, REG-721).
- **L-221** · **Keys**: no key handler of your own — mark controls `data-kb`, lists `data-kb-list` / `data-kb-row` / `data-kb-open` / `data-kb-select`; a new key goes into the registry first; nothing that reaches a client, publishes or deletes forever gets a key (`page-checklist.md:50-62`; REG-711…717).
- **L-222** · **An AI press says its price first** and is charged at the work with a keyed ref, refunded on failure or Cancel (`page-checklist.md:218`; REG-704).
- **L-223** · **Server actions**: live in `app/**/actions.ts`, end with `revalidatePath`; a long string (a picture, rich text) travels inside one object argument; never write to `local.db` from a test (`CONTRIBUTING.md:89-102`).

**Every width**

- **L-224** · Breakpoints: phone ≤767 · tablet 768–1024 · desktop ≥1025; only two media-query literals; **desktop stays pixel-identical at 1440**; the page never scrolls sideways (gate also at 320 and 360) (`responsive.md:8-16`, `:85-94`; REG-269, REG-315).
- **L-225** · On a phone every anchored surface is a bottom sheet (alerts stay centred, multi-step wizards stay full screen), a section toolbar is one row, hover-revealed controls are permanent, touch boxes are ≥44 (`page-checklist.md:154-167`; REG-312, REG-353, REG-354).
- **L-226** · A level-1 page ends with `--fab-clearance`, registers its search with `useSearchTarget`, and bottom-anchored things read `--bottom-dock` / `--app-mobile-dock-height` (`page-checklist.md:28-36`, `:146-148`).

**The checks**

- **L-227** · `npm run design:lint` counts violations per file against `design-lint.baseline.json`, which may only go down. The script defines **nine** rule ids — overlay-head · close-button · raw-colour · raw-type · raw-radius · inline-style · ds-bypass · row-fade · copied-table (`scripts/design-lint.mjs:52-122`); `CLAUDE.md` says eight.
