# 4 · Finance (1.0's project Finance tab) — what 2.0 still lacks

Reviewer 4 of 7 · read only · 2026-10-07 · inventory `projects-1.0-inventory/G-finance-tab.md` (G-01…G-520), 1.0 `qanvast-id-tool` @ `620190192`,
2.0 = the working tree of `openmaison-prototype`. Nothing was run in either product; every 2.0 status is read from its code.

## Bottom line

**Money in is largely built, and built better than 1.0's.** The Money tab has the contract value (signed quote + client-approved VOs), one invoice per payment stage made as a Draft with the project, the rebalance with Undo, invoice approval, Send by WhatsApp, Void with a reason, payments with proof → Confirm / Not received / Reverse, numbered receipts, overdue with *Remind on WhatsApp*, and the client's Money page in the portal with a PayNow QR. It also leaves several 1.0 faults behind: three sets of numbers, no money gate, no way to reverse a payment, and a payment proof the studio could never see. Of the 520 rows, **183 land in Built lines (29), 57 in Missing or Partly lines (6 Missing · 8 Partly), 217 in Left out lines (19) and 63 are not product features (5)**.

The money-in gaps cluster around **billing anything the stage list doesn't hold**:
- A variation order approved after every stage has been invoiced has no invoice to go on (High).
- There's no one-off (ad-hoc) invoice.
- An invoice is one fixed line, with no lines of its own, no note to the client and no due date of its own.
- Completing a milestone doesn't prompt the studio to send its invoice.
- A client who isn't on the portal gets no invoice or receipt link.

**Set aside by accepted calls:** credit notes (F-09), the over-bill / under-bill treatments and top-ups (REG-999), changing the agreed split mid-project (F-03 · F-08), the Accounting role (F-16) and the studio-wide finance pages (F-01 · F-19).

**Money out and the books are the biggest block, 170 rows, and wholly absent by Q-35 (later).** It covers POs with a vendor portal and advances, expenses with reimbursement claims and phone capture, supplier bills read by AI, vendors, cost / profit / margin, commission, AI Insights, reports, and 1.0's Xero sync (not in the inventory).

## Top gaps

1. **A VO approved after every stage has been invoiced can't be billed.** Client-approved money is left with no invoice; the only way round (void and re-send) fails once invoices are paid.
2. **No prompt to send the invoice when a milestone is completed.** "Ready to send" sits quietly on Money, so studios can bill late and cash comes in late.
3. **No one-off (ad-hoc) invoice.** Reimbursements, upgrades and small extras can't be billed without inventing a VO.
4. **An invoice is one fixed line.** There are no lines, description or note to the client, so a progress claim can't explain itself on the paper the client keeps.
5. **No invoice or receipt link for a client who isn't on the portal.** The studio downloads and attaches the PDF by hand, and invoices aren't kept in Files.
6. **An invoice can't have its own due date.** Studio-wide terms only.
7. **VO omissions are typed by hand as minus lines.** There's no "Omit from contract" picker from the signed quote's lines and no reason field, which leaves room for errors on credits.
8. **No "collection behind progress" warning, and no "draft left unsent" reason.** These are the early signals that cash is slipping.
9. **Chasing has no memory on the row.** There's no "reminded 3×", no age line and no escalation.
10. **No way to log an invoice made outside OpenMaison.** Its money counts nowhere.

(By volume, the far larger absence is money out and the books — POs, expenses, bills, vendors, profit / margin, commission, AI Insights, reports and accounting sync — set aside by Q-35 as "later". So are credit notes, F-09.)
