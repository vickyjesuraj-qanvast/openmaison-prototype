1. **Image routes have no access check.** Check `/visualizer/img/{room|v|m|d}/…` and `/files/img/[fileId]`. They never ask Who sees leads, whether the design is deleted, or whether a share link is stopped or expired. On top of that, `v/…` is served `public, max-age=31536000, immutable`. So once a link is stopped, or a version or design is deleted, anyone holding the address still gets the client's photo, and proxies may cache it. Check this with curl after a stop, a delete and a delete-forever, and as View as Sarah on a lead she can't see. The fix is to gate each read on the policy and use `private` caching for anything that isn't a public sample.

2. **The WhatsApp link preview was never tested.** `generateMetadata` in `app/renders/[token]/page.tsx` only sets a title. The client's first touchpoint has no `og:image` (the first shared render), no `og:description` (studio, lead, how many renders) and no studio logo, so WhatsApp shows a bare URL. A world-class share should unfurl. Build an OG image route that follows the link's state, so a stopped link doesn't show the render.

3. **The UAT story has no visualizer chapter.** `ch1.json` only reaches the Tools tile (step 1.06). `ch2`…`ch12` never open the visualizer. The 111 rows in `checklist-VIZ.json` are a parity checklist, not a walk. The story needs a chapter, or steps woven into Kevin Ng's lead chapters, that follows a real flow:
   - open the lead's design, pick walls and floor, Generate
   - compare, present, moodboard
   - Save to Files, Add to a quote
   - share to the client, then play the client: like, pin, call back
   - see the bell entry, the Activity line and the task
   - stop the link.

   The xlsx then needs a rebuild, and the owner already flagged that step 1.06 is stale.

4. **The lead's lifecycle and its design were not covered.** No lens checked what happens to the lead's design and a live `/renders` link when the lead:
   - is Returned to Qanvast
   - goes Lost or On Hold
   - is deleted, then deleted forever (FK cascade on `lead_id` drops the design and its links, and the saved renders in Files may be orphaned)
   - goes Won and becomes a project
   - has its name edited (design title, share page greeting, file names)
   - has its phone number changed after a link was made (the wa.me number).

   `app/leads/actions.ts` and `return-actions.ts` have no visualizer hooks. A returned lead's client link staying open is the main concern.

5. **Dark mode was checked nowhere** (every lens skipped it). The editor, landing and Materials use `--om-*` tokens, but the stage chrome, pin cards, markers, compare handle and moodboard print styles need a pass. The client page `/renders` sits outside AppShell, so decide whether it follows the system theme or stays light, and check the studio accent on dark.

   - it applies on the phone
   - "Reset prototype to base" clears all `om.viz.*` keys: hint, introHidden, recent, markersHidden.

   - Reset after renders were saved to Files, then confirm Files › Renders, `lead_file_images` and the Kevin Ng design come back consistent, with no orphan `render_jobs` or `renders` files.
   - `npm run db:repair` has visualizer rules. Run it on a DB where a lens deleted Kevin's design forever and check it re-seeds cleanly (REG-343 trail: Activity lines, `lead_events`).
   - The baseline JSON size, given base64 renders inside `variants_json`.

8. **Maison AI inside the editor.** The editor mounts `MaisonFab` on its own, and only the landing calls `useMaisonAi`. Check what the sheet offers inside a design. It has no scripted suggestions such as "Suggest a palette for this lead's style", "Describe this render for the client" or "Draft the WhatsApp message". Also check that it works on the read-only or deleted state.

9. **Two tabs and two members on one design.** Autosave writes `state_json` wholesale, so the last writer wins. No lens opened the same design in two tabs, or as Dane and Hui Min, to see picks overwrite each other, V-numbers collide, or one tab delete a room the other is rendering into. At minimum, detect a stale `updatedAt` and say "Changed elsewhere, reload".

10. **The network failing mid-Generate.** `saveRender` catches errors, but check these by throttling or blocking the action:
    - offline during the progress card
    - a server 500 on `saveRender` (is the in-flight tile removed? are picks kept?)
    - a very slow save while the user leaves (no `beforeunload` or `pagehide` guard exists)
    - a retry that doesn't double-save.

11. **Download quality and export options.** Every download is a 900×603 JPEG. A studio sending renders to clients or printing boards expects:
    - full resolution, or at least the photo's native size
    - PNG as an option
    - "Download all versions" or a room as a zip
    - an optional studio logo or watermark, taken from the brand kit.

    Also check that the download from the client page (if offered) keeps the studio's naming.

12. **Version provenance.** No lens checked whether a version can say exactly what made it: which picks, which materials with their Price book links, style, light knobs, notes, and parent.
