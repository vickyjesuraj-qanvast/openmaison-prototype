=== FAQ:
Q1 [default expanded=true]: What is a token?
A: A unit of AI. One render is 10 tokens, digitizing a quote is 5, ten minutes of Meeting Co-Pilot is 25. Every button shows its cost before you press it, so nothing is spent by surprise. Everything that is not AI — leads, projects, quotes, finance — is unmetered on every plan.
Q2 [default expanded=false]: Do I need a card to start?
A: No. You get 150 tokens on signup and 30 a month after that, with no card and no expiry date on the account. The tools also run before you sign up at all. Pro's free trial does take a card, because it continues as a paid plan unless you cancel.
Q3 [default expanded=false]: Is there a free trial of Pro?
A: Yes — 14 days on monthly Pro, Freelance or Studio. We take a card up front and charge only after the trial ends; cancel before then and you pay nothing. You get the full Pro token allowance during the trial. It's one trial per studio, and annual plans don't include one.
Q4 [default expanded=false]: What happens when I run out of tokens?
A: The AI actions pause; nothing else does. Your leads, quotes, projects and finance keep working exactly as before. You can top up 1,000 tokens for S$20, or move up a plan — and topped-up tokens never expire.
Q5 [default expanded=false]: Why is Studio three seats?
A: Studio is priced for a team, so it starts at three licences and pools 4,500 tokens across them — any designer can draw from the same allowance. Extra seats are S$35 each per month. If you work alone, Freelance is the same product at one seat.
Q6 [default expanded=false]: Can I change plans later?
A: Anytime, in either direction. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the end of your billing period, so you keep what you have already paid for.
Q7 [default expanded=false]: Do you support my region?
A: OpenMaison is built for Singapore, Malaysia, the UAE and India, with local taxes, phone formats and property types inside the product. Prices are published in Singapore dollars.
