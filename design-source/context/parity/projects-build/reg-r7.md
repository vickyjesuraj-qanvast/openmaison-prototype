| REG-1019 | Projects · Round 7 | **Complete handover, one ending** (Q-24 · Q-25; 1.0's Close · Archive · Delete overlap and its no-way-back left behind, D-308 · D-309 · D-321…D-326). The stage's one filled action once the work is near its end (`nearHandover`: at most one milestone left, or no schedule and the target end within 7 days — `NEAR_END_DAYS`, invented threshold) and a row in the project's menu at any time while In progress. A **checklist drawn from the records, never ticked by hand** (`lib/projects/handover-model.ts` `handoverChecklist`, facts from `lib/projects/handover.ts` `handoverFactsOf`): every milestone done · the handover stage (the milestone that ends last, `handoverOf`) confirmed by the client — only when the studio asked them (Q-16) · nothing outstanding on money (read through the money module only — `moneyInputOf` · `moneyOf`: outstanding, invoices waiting for approval, the contract not invoiced while payment stages exist) · no update waiting for review. Each unmet item says why and carries its own button to the tab that fixes it (Open the schedule · Open the stage · Open Money · Open Updates, keeping `?from=`). The dialog is the house `Dialog` (m 600) over the card table (Settings' event-table look); it loads the checklist on open (`readHandover`), so the listing's row and the project page share it, and closes only once its save lands. | user-directed |
| REG-1020 | Projects · Round 7 | **Complete anyway** — an Owner or an Admin completes with items still open, and must say why (`completesAnyway`; "Say why it is completed with items open"); the reason and the open items' labels stay on the record (`projects.complete_reason`, the `project_completed` event's `open` and `reason`) and read in Activity: "Dane completed the handover — the project is completed with 2 items open (…) — “…”". A Designer or a Manager with items open is told "Finish what’s left — or ask an Owner or an Admin to complete it anyway" and the press stays off. | user-directed · invented copy |
