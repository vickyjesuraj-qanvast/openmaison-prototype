## Summary — 24 findings (Blocker 1 · Major 3 · Minor 14 · Polish 6)

What's solid: the eight steps did what their outcomes say, and most of it holds up under a real walk. Every new surface reaches from the
place a studio person already looks (the stage menu's *Manage Stages ›*, the quote's ⋮, the lead's ⋮ › Quote X-Ray, Contracts ⋮ › Contracts
Settings, the Brand kit's paper door) and Back returns there, at 1440 and on a phone. Nothing scrolls sideways at 390, 320 or 768 touch. The new
dialogs (Appeal, Match lines, Draft with Maison AI, Write with AI's proposal, Upload a standard PDF, Upload a font, Upload your existing quote,
Share a view-only link) are house dialogs that become bottom sheets on a phone, and they take Escape and ⌘↵. The client projection holds: a
client quote page carries the line's price (cost and mark-up folded together), never the cost, mark-up, rebate or vendor (checked in the HTML
of `/confirm` and `/sign/<token>/quote`). Writing help is free, works by rule, and shows a proposal with Undo every time. X-Ray reads 1.0's
competitor PDF whole (23 lines, S$40,210.00), its figures add up on the record, and it works for a Designer on a phone. Old 1.0 links
redirect where the outcome says. Link cards carry the studio's name. Refusals for Accounting are house gates with their reason.

What's wrong, in short: (1) a client can still **sign an agreement while the studio is revising its quote**. The draft revision becomes the
signed quote. (2) **"Viewed" counts the studio's own visits as the client's.** (3) The contract signing pages still say **a code was sent** when
none is. (4) The Lead stages card **ignores Who sees leads**.
