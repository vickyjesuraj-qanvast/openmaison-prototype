## 2 · On the move

### The listing on a phone — the benchmark, and what it should become
Jobs (on site, in the car, between meetings): (1) see what is new since this morning and answer it inside two hours; (2) who do I owe a call
today — overdue follow-ups first; (3) call or WhatsApp one lead in one tap and log it in ten seconds; (4) move a lead after a visit; (5) hand a
lead to a colleague.
Today: the canvas's cards do (3)'s call and (5) well. (1) has no mark (A-01); (2) needs the pill → Overdue and then guesses dates (A-03); (3) has no
WhatsApp and no "log it" after the call (A-05); (4) is two taps into a 7-row list, and blocked moves dead-end (A-06, A-07); the FAB can eat the
stage tap (A-02).
**Phone — a call sheet.** Header as today (+ filled · Insights · ⋮). Toolbar as today (pill · search · filter). Then the cards in the canvas's
anatomy with three changes: a **New** mark on unseen leads; **line 2** phone (dials) · WhatsApp glyph · budget; **line 3** "Last contact 3d ago ·
Next: Mon 10:00" (red when ours and late). Foot: the stage pill on the left (no FAB collision), members on the right. The stage sheet opens with the
next move as a filled row ("Move to Contacted"), a blocked move says why with its fix inline ("Assign a member first · Assign"). After a call made
from the card, coming back to the app shows a one-line snackbar "Called Farah Ahmad · Log it" that opens the Contacted log (or a Notes contact log
past Contacted) — the ten-second log. Add Task gets Today · Tomorrow · Pick a date. Behind a tap (the card → the lead): source, days in stage,
address, budget detail.

### The listing on a tablet — and the kanban
Jobs: the same five, plus a morning review of the pipeline (who is stuck in which stage) and moving several leads after a team meeting.
Today: 768 shows three columns and 7 rows a screen (A-12), actions are a screen and a half away (A-13), the board can't move a lead (A-15).
**768 portrait** follows the phone: the cards, two up. **1024 landscape**: the table with a tablet default — Lead Name · Stage · Next follow-up · Tasks
· Members, ⋮ frozen after the name, 64 rows (10 a screen); selection + the bulk bar as today. **Kanban** at both: long-press lifts a card into
the drag; the card's ⋮ gains Move to {next} · Move stage…; 768 portrait snaps 300 columns like the phone.

### The popovers from a card or a row (stage · Tasks · Assign)
Jobs: move it, remind myself, hand it over — each without leaving the list. The three sheets are right in shape (REG-353); they need the lead named
on all three (A-08), the next move first and refusals with their fix (A-06, A-07), a due date on a quick task (A-09). On a tablet they stay
anchored (they fit: 200×399, 400×311, 400×440).

### Create New Lead (+ Fill from a document, the duplicate)
Jobs (a call coming in, a WhatsApp enquiry forwarded by the boss, a walk-in at a show flat): capture name + phone in seconds, fill the rest from
what was sent, never make a duplicate, never lose it.
Today the two required fields are first (right), but a ✕ loses everything (A-17), the fill speaks of dropping files and takes none of a phone's
own inputs (A-19), the safe duplicate answer is the outlined one (A-20), and Additional info is 2.8 screens in one run (A-18).
**Phone.** The sheet opens on Name · Mobile (16, autofill hints, the phone pad), then **Paste a message** / **Choose a file** as two rows (a pasted
WhatsApp enquiry fills the empty fields and says which, as today's fill line does), then Source · Property type · Assign, then Additional info as
named folds. Create (48 in the foot). A typed form closed by ✕ answers "Lead discarded · Undo". The duplicate check offers Open existing lead
(filled). After Create the lead opens (today it only snackbars "Successfully Created" and the person scrolls the list to find it) with Call first —
or the snackbar carries "Open". From search, an unknown number already starts this with the phone filled (keep).
**Tablet.** Today's m 600 dialog is right; the same Paste a message row, the same guard.

### Import + Review imported leads
Jobs on a phone are rare and specific: a Homematch PDF or a sheet someone sent — import it, see what is wrong, approve the clean ones, settle a
duplicate. Today the phone does the desktop's four steps at desktop density: 5.9 screens of mapping for a file that matched itself (A-22), a 12-column
preview that hides the one problem (A-23), and a merge of 15 fields × 3 rows for one differing phone (A-24).
**Phone.** Step 1 = one "Choose a file" button and the kinds it reads; step 2 collapses to "11 of 11 columns matched · Check"; step 3 = cards with the
problems in red; the routing counts what will land; step 4 names what was skipped and why. Review: the queue as cards (grouped phones, 14), the merge
as its own screen with only the differing fields, the queue toolbar gone while merging; Approve All Clean answers with Undo.
