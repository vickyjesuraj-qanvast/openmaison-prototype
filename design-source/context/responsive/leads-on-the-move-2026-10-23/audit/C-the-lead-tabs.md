spends 241px on the same header and a nine-tab strip that hides half its tabs. *Phone:* the lead's screen (Overview, Lane B) ends with the

Back works). This is K1's `SectionList` → section screen, with a lead's words. *Tablet portrait:* the same. *Landscape 1024:* the rows as a
300 pane beside the open tab, as the Quote screen's landscape list.
