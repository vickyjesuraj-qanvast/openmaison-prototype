## Decisions taken (as recommended; flagged for him)

1. **The section screen's "Done" is its ←.** P-02's text says "a full-screen sheet with Done"; the drawn screen 4 (Items) draws ← alone, with the section's own filled action (Add item) in its foot. A second filled Done beside it would break one-filled-button-per-screen; Done lives on the list's bar, where it returns to the Quote screen. ← is labelled "Back" with the tooltip "Back to the sections"; Esc does the same on a keyboard tablet.
2. **Remind writes nothing.** There is no reminder record on a quote (contracts have `reminders`; quotes do not) and no schema change was expected. Remind opens the house send dialog as "Send a reminder" with a WhatsApp draft carrying the same confirmation link; the snackbar says "Reminder sent to Serena on WhatsApp". Activity does not list it. If a record is wanted, it is an additive column + an Activity branch (R-rule) — one line to ask.
3. **The ⋮ keeps every desk action, grouped.** B-09 asked for ≤10 rows; the brief's principle "Nothing is lost, it is one tap further" wins: 16 rows under four group titles, a sheet that scrolls (REG-353). Preview and Comments left the sheet (they are on the screen); Edit template and Change template joined it (the phone's template card lost them, B-24).
4. **One `!important`.** The foot's Send ▾ on a 768–1024 tablet is K5's `SendButton` over the house `Dropdown`, which places its menu inline (`top: calc(100% + 4px)`); at the bottom of the screen that runs off the edge. `quote-screen.module.css` overrides `top` / `bottom` / `transform-origin` for `.primary [role='menu']` at 768–1024 — the one place an inline value is overridden; a `side` prop on `SendButton` (K5) would remove it.
5. **The schedule prints once.** The document's tail passes `schedule={null}` to `ReadingCards`: the Quote screen's own Payment schedule row holds the rows with their amounts and dues.
6. **A finger keeps its box.** The screen takes focus once, as it opens (`isScreen`, a boolean) — the first cut refocused the screen whenever the bar's total changed, which would have pulled the keyboard off a price being typed in a line's sheet; the probe now types a price and checks.
7. **The hidden sections stay mounted** (`display: none`), never unmounted: the Items table, the schedule and the texts hold their state, so the Quote screen's amount, lines, summaries and the live client view never drift from what was edited, and a section opened again is exactly as it was left.
8. **The words on the list and the bars** are the drawn screen's ("General info" · "Payment schedule" · "Terms and conditions" · "Additional notes"); the desk's cards keep the mock's Title Case.
9. **Signed, no contract, lead before Won → Move to Won** opens the Move stage window at Won with this quote picked (`moveStageHref(lead, 'won', { quote })`).
