## 2 · On the move

### Tools › Quotes (`/quotes`)
Jobs on site / in a car / at a client's home: (1) what is waiting on me or on a client today; (2) find a client's quote and open it; (3) the amount and the status before a call; (4) copy the link or nudge the client; (5) start a quote for the lead I'm with.
Today the phone makes them: read seven identical "Whole-home renovation" titles with the client's name clipped (A-01), tap a 20px title to open (A-05), guess the dates (A-07), dodge a six-row status changer (A-08) and a FAB over the figure (A-06), and open a ⋮ sheet of eight rows to copy a link. The tablet shows a 3.3-screen table whose amount and actions are off to the right (A-02, A-03) and a chip that says "3" (A-04).
**Phone.** Top: the title, "+" (the one primary), search. A to-do strip with words: "Waiting on client 3 · Expiring this week 1 · For my approval 2" (chips, scroll sideways). Then cards, the whole card a press with a chevron: line 1 the client's name (bold 16) with the amount right; line 2 "Whole-home renovation · OM-10007"; line 3 a read-only status chip + "Sent 16 Sep · not viewed" or "Due Fri 24 Oct". ⋮ at the top right opens: Open · Copy link · Remind on WhatsApp · Preview · Change status › · Archive · Delete. No checkbox; no FAB over a figure (the figure moves up). Filters = one sheet of chip rows. Nothing else.
**Tablet portrait (768).** The phone's cards two-up, or the same table with a tablet default (Title · Client · Status · Total · Due, Title + ⋮ frozen) — everything a person needs on the first screen, Column settings for the rest. **Landscape (1024).** The table with that default plus Sent · Valid until, ⋮ frozen; the drawer as it is.
