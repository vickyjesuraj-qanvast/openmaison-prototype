## 2 · On the move

### The Items section on a phone (390)
**Jobs:** (1) read the lines and the total with the client beside you; (2) change a quantity or a unit price while standing in the room; (3) add
a line you just agreed ("add 2 more downlights"); (4) tick a few lines and move or remove them; (5) send — lane B's header.
**What today makes them do:** scroll a 6,832px page to the Items card (C-23); read 108px cards whose figures are 12px (C-13); tap a card's
text (fine) and land in a 14-field sheet that asks for cost and markup before the price (C-05); to add a line, tap "+" → a sheet → New Item
→ then the typed box at the bottom of the list under the sticky head (C-10); to remove, open a 16-row ⋮ and scroll to Remove (C-06) or
tick and find Delete off the end of a sideways bulk bar (C-07); to see the total, scroll 1,300px past By category (C-08). The two
smallest targets on the screen are the ones a finger uses most (C-03, C-04).
**What it should be:** the Items tab is a screen. Top: "Items · 16 · S$49,838" with **Add item** (filled) and search. The list: one card per
line — name, "681 sqft × S$4.00", the total — about 72px; sections as full-width tinted headers that fold on tap, with "Add to Bathrooms" at
their end. Pinned at the bottom: **Subtotal · GST · Total** (one strip; "Adjust" opens entity · discounts · GST). A tap on a line opens a
sheet whose first screen is Name · Qty × Unit · Unit price · Total (live) · Description and a "More" fold. The ⋮ has five rows. Tick → a bar
with Move · Delete · ⋯. Nothing on this screen mentions columns, density, Expand, Copy or By category.

### The Items grid on a tablet (768 portrait · 1024 landscape)
**Jobs:** the same five, plus (6) walk the quote line by line with a client at a table and correct prices as you go.
**What today makes them do:** at 768, pan a 2,067px row through a 62px window to reach any number (C-01) — the grid is not usable for job 2
or 6 in portrait; at 1024, pan 400–800px per price (C-02) with two rows visible once the keyboard is up; mis-tap Create for Maison AI (C-03).
**What it should be — 768 portrait:** the phone's cards (they exist) with a **right-hand line panel** (the LineSheet's fields, 400 wide) so the
list stays in view while a line is edited: tap a card → the panel fills, Qty and Unit price first. The summary strip pinned.
**1024 landscape:** a grid can work if it is a *working* grid: Name · Qty · Unit · Unit price · Total (642px) in a 902px card with no pan,
the frozen blocks reduced to the name, and Cost · Markup · Margin · Discount · GST · Type · Category · Section · Room behind Column
settings remembered per breakpoint. Or the same cards + panel as 768 — at 1024 the panel has room for every field without scrolling.

### The summary (totals · discounts · By category)
**Jobs:** say the total; apply the discount you just agreed; check GST.
**Today:** 1,020px of block (C-08) with 12px inputs (C-09). **Should be:** three lines pinned; "Adjust" for the rest; By category is a
desktop analysis (REG-532) — a fold on a tablet, absent on a phone.

### The Preview (390 · 768 · 1024)
**Jobs:** (1) show the client the quote on your phone or hand them the tablet; (2) check what the client sees before Send; (3) send.
**Today:** at 390 a 4.4px paper under a 234px sticky bar (C-16, C-17) — jobs 1 and 2 fail; at 768 an 8.5–10px paper (C-18); only 1024 reads.
"What your client sees" is a scrolling drawer of 40px rows (C-19); Copy link copies a team-only URL (C-20).
**Should be — phone:** the quote as the client's own reflowed page (the confirm page already proves it at 390: `shots/m390-12-confirm-full.png`),
one 56px bar (Back · Preview · Send), a "Paper" toggle for the PDF at 1:1 with sideways scroll, the ⋮ for Copy link · Share PDF · Internal
copy · What your client sees · Change template. **Tablet portrait:** the paper zoomed to its text width (zoom 1, margins scrolling), the
same one-line bar. **Tablet landscape:** as today at zoom 1, with the bar on one line.

### The Client view pane on the editor (≤1024)
**Job:** glance at what the client gets while editing. **Today:** a 390/400px drawer of 4.4px text over the editor (C-21). **Should be:**
≤1024 the button opens Preview (one tap, the same component), and the pane exists only at ≥1025 where it was designed (REG-497).
