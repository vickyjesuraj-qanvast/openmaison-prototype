# Lane W5 — the tools: the audit (2026-10-23 → 24, overnight)

**Scope (brief §4):** Tools › Quotes (the list, the Quote screen, the editor and its section screens, Preview, Create Quote, the Send dialogs),
Tools › Contracts (the list, the Contract screen, the editor, the Document screen, Preview, the template picker), the client's links
(`/sign/<token>` + bundles + `/sign/<token>/quote`, `/confirm/<token>`, `/quote/<token>`), Quote X-Ray (setup, a reading, the send dialog), the
AI Visualizer (home, the design editor), the 3D Space Planner (home grid, the editor — not `planner-home.tsx`), the Marketplace, Market Insights.

**How it was looked at.** The sweep (`out/sweep.json`) for my routes, then three passes of my own on the base build (`rwd-all/W5/`):
`explore.cjs` (36 routes × 320 · 390 · 768↑ · 1024↔ · 844×390 touch · 1280 · 1440 mouse, the sweep's measure, top + end shots, contact sheets),
`crawl.cjs` (each route × width with **every scroller at its end** — the sweep only scrolled the document — an *effective-target* test (where a
finger 21px off a control's centre lands) and every popup control opened by a real tap / click: ⋮, Send ▾, status, New, Insert, View, Kind,
Sort…), `flows.cjs` (the presses past the first menu: Create Quote, the Send dialogs, the line sheet, Add item, Adjust, Breakdown, a new
section, the phone payment sheet, New contract → a lead → the picker, Mark as signed, Insert, the signing sheet's screens, the Approve / Confirm
sheets, Ask for a change, the X-Ray ⋮ → a correction, Send to client, the Visualizer's five trays, a marker, Share, the planner's panels and
Rename, a marketplace deal) and `ghosts.cjs` (the held reveal, pm2-s17's recipe, ghost beside page at five widths). The sweep's flags were each
checked by a tap; where the sweep was wrong it says so below.

Severity: **blocker** — a person cannot do the thing · **major** — a control covered, cut or unreachable at rest · **minor** — a small target,
a stray line, a ghost off the page's shape.
