## 2. On the move — what each surface should be

### The contract (Draft · Sent to Client) — phone, and a tablet in portrait
**Jobs on site, in a car, at the client's home (3–5):** (1) see who it is for, what it says and what it is waiting on; (2) fix the four things that change on the day — the recipient and their number (the code goes there), the contract date, the quote it names (the amount chips follow), the odd sentence; (3) make sure the client's signature box is there; (4) send it on WhatsApp — or copy the link into the chat already open; (5) see whether it was opened, remind, and know when the link closes.
**What today makes them do:** scroll 520–640 px of chrome to reach the first word (B-06); edit prose under a two-row bar with seven tools hidden in a swipe (B-03), while the FAB sits on the text (B-20); open a full-height sheet to place a field that lands at the end or inside a word (B-04, B-05); scroll back to the top for Send (B-07); read a five-line banner to learn the link's state.
**What it should be — the Contract screen** (the Quote screen's shape, `components/quotes/screen/*`, REG-2525…2533, generalised):
- **Bar (56):** ← (back where you came from) · *Harpreet Kaur* over "Interior Design Service Agreement · from OM-10006" · ⋮ (grouped, B-22).
- **Hero (card, 12 inside):** the status pill (today's — every status rule holds) · *From template* · the contract date as a pressable row ("Contract date · 22 Oct ’26") · the quote row ("OM-10006 · S$67,392.52 · Approved", press → the quote sheet) · the client row (avatar · *Harpreet Kaur* · 9134 3428 · Call · WhatsApp · ✎ Edit recipient). Signed adds "Signed 31 Jul ’26 · by Ryan D’Souza online / marked by the studio".
- **One Next line:** Draft → "Next: send it to Harpreet for signature — the client signature box is in place" (or the readiness blocker: "No client signature field yet — add one"). Sent → "With Olivia for signature, sent 20 min ago · not opened yet · link open until 29 Oct". Signed → "Signed 31 Jul ’26 · the quote is signed through it · the lead is still in Quote Sent". `lib/contracts/next-step.ts`, pure, on `nextStep`'s model.
- **The document as reading cards:** `ContractPaper`'s reflowed body (already 16 px, 324 wide) split at its headings — the Outline's 12 entries become folds ("1. Scope of works", "2. Contract sum" with the first open), the signature boxes a last card "Signatures · Studio signature · Client signature — placed". No toolbar, no caret.
- **Folded rows (56):** Signature fields (Studio · Client · initials — placed / not placed; Add) · Before the client signs (the two switches, their words) · Automation (two switches) · Files / Signed copy · Activity.
- **Sticky foot (48):** **Edit** · **Send** (Draft) / **Remind** (Sent) / Download signed copy · Move to Won (Signed; no Edit — the copy is kept, REG-631).
- **Edit → a short section list** (`SectionList`): Details (title · recipient · date · quote · issued by — one form sheet) · Document (opens the paper full-screen) · Signature fields · Before the client signs · Automation. Each row with its one-line summary ("Harpreet Kaur · 9134 3428 · 22 Oct ’26"). The address carries it (`?edit=document`) so Back works (REG-465).
- **The Document screen** (REG-2574's full-screen editor, `Dialog phoneFull`): a 56 bar (← Document · Done) and **one 44-pitch tool row**: Undo · Text style ▾ · B · I · U · List · **+ ▾** (a sheet: Insert a field › · Add a signature field › · Link · Image · Highlight · Alignment · Numbered list) — nothing scrolls sideways, nothing hidden. ### Where Contracts reuses Quotes, and where it differs
Reuse as they are: `ScreenBar`, the Quote screen's hero / fold / foot stylesheet (lift into a `RecordScreen` or share the module), `SectionList`, `PaperViewer`, `quote-preview.module.css .touchBar / .foot`, `SendContractDialog` (already shared), `Dialog phoneFull` + the 44-pitch tool row of `rich-editor.module.css`, the ≤767 radio-list picker (REG-2579), `useSheetFootLift`. Differs: the document is **prose with signature boxes** — cards are clauses by heading, not lines with amounts, and the body is `ContractPaper`'s reflow, not `ReadingCards`; the **placement of a field** is a decision a finger must make from a list, not a caret; **Signed is frozen** (no Edit in the foot; the kept copy, REG-631); the **recipient and number** are first-class (the one-time code), so the hero row is the client with Edit, not just Call and WhatsApp; the **link's life** (7 days, opened / not opened, Stop the link) is a state the Next line must say; the client's signing page (Lane A) is the page that must be perfect — the editor's job on a phone is to get a correct document to it in under a minute. The loading ghost (`contract-skeleton.tsx`) mirrors today's chrome-first layout and must change with the screen (REG-708).
