## 2 · Decisions taken (as recommended; the ones he should see flagged ▲)

1. ▲ **The Document screen takes the whole width on a landscape tablet** (not the 724 beside the list pane): the paper is the point of that screen, and with the Outline open as a 300 pane at its left it still stands at 610 — beside the list pane it would have been 412. ← brings the list back. Details and the other sections keep the list beside them.
2. ▲ **The quote row opens the quote sheet with "Open OM-10001" as its first row.** The brief's "→ the quote sheet" is the picker; a person on site more often wants the quote itself, so the sheet offers both (the picker's rows under it). Signed, the row opens the quote directly (the quote stays as signed).
3. ▲ **Signature fields' Add places at the end** (C1's interim): the brief gives the placement sheet — *which*, then *where* — to C2; until it lands, Add puts the field as its own block after the last clause (never inside a word, B-05's rule) and says so with Undo, and the Signature fields screen's note says a field can be moved from the Document screen. C2 swaps `onAdd` for the placement sheet; nothing else changes.
4. **The Signed hero's quote row has no picker** and the date row no chevron: a signed copy names what it signed.
5. ▲ **The Send sheet "fits" is a rule about its fixed rows.** B-17's four changes are in; what a contract adds — the "Before it goes" note (Olivia's quote is a Draft an approver's send approves), the standard documents' line, the deposit row, and the signing link's length in the message (localhost's is two lines longer than a real domain's) — can still push the body to scroll a little under the pinned foot, whose buttons never leave the fold (K5 wrote the same of the quote's). The probe asserts the fixed rows fit and the primary stays in view. If he wants it to fit whatever a contract carries, the message box would have to scroll inside the sheet again — the thing K5 removed.
6. **Remind writes the link's renewal**, as the lists do (1.0's resend: `renewSigningLink(id, 'whatsapp')` logs `contract_reminded`), so the Activity fold and the bell see it — the quote's Remind had no record to write; the contract's has.
7. **The Activity fold reads the record's own stamps** (created · sent · opened · ticked · signed), not the lead's event log — `ContractEditorData` carries no team or event list, and a second query for a fold was not worth a schema-side change. Honest data: nothing invented.
8. **`?edit=` on a Signed contract is ignored**: Signed has no Edit (REG-631); Automation on a Signed contract opens its dialog from ⋮ (the desk's folded kebab's way).
9. **Edit recipient on the move is Details** (one form, the recipient fields focused) — the desk's RecipientDialog stays the desk's.
10. **`TABLET_LANDSCAPE`** named once in `lib/breakpoint.ts` (K1's `SPLIT` literal) — see Shared pieces.
11. **Words**: "As the client reads it" / "The copy the client signed" (the Preview's row), "Paper" (the toggle), "How signing works", the folds' lines — all staged as invented copy in `reg-c1.md`.
12. ▲ **The desk's Preview now holds its ghost through `PageReveal`** (the one desk-side change): the loader streams with the address's shape, and the ghosts regression (`ghosts-same` at 1440) found the contract Preview was the one editor page with no reveal to hold it — the quote's Preview has had one since sweep S1 #27 (page-checklist › Load & refresh). The loaded frame is byte-identical (§7); a cold load shows the house ghost beat every other page shows.
13. **"How signing works" is the contract sheet's** (`subtitleShort` passed by the editor and the Preview): the first cut folded every `SendContractDialog`, and rq-k5 found the quote's Send for confirmation — which fitted exactly — scrolling 20px with a fold it never needed, under a head that said "signs". Only a host that passes the short line gets the fold.
