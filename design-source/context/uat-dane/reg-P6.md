**The snackbar rule (for DESIGN.md › interaction rules — Vicky 2026-10-01):**
A snackbar confirms what just happened and may carry one next step or Undo. It is never the only place something you must act on is said:
a warning that should change a decision lives inline, where the decision is made. It stays 4 s for a plain one-line message and 8 s when
it carries an action or its words wrap; the clock stops while the pointer rests on it, while a finger is held on it and while focus is
inside it, then runs on with what was left (never less than 2 s). Its action keeps to one line — the message wraps instead.
